Design language

Every line on the RS 3 communicates purpose. The widened front track, flared arches, and gloss black inlets channel airflow to the intercoolers while signaling the RS badge's motorsport pedigree.

Inside, high-contrast stitching, RS-exclusive carbon accents, and supportive sport seats transform the cabin into a focused command center without sacrificing premium materials.

Signature styling cues

Audi's designers created an RS 3 that looks as sharp as it drives. Details inspired by racing disciplines elevate both aesthetics and functionality.

  • Matrix LED headlights with digital daytime running light signatures.
  • Optional RS design package with red or green seat belts and air vent accents.
  • Lightweight 19-inch wheels wrapped in staggered performance tires for optimal grip.

Driver-centric cockpit

RS sport seats with pronounced bolsters, honeycomb stitching, and integrated headrests hold the driver securely during spirited drives.

Materials that matter

Carbon fiber inlays, Dinamica micro-suede, and brushed aluminum trim create tactile contrast while reducing weight.

Functional aerodynamics

Rear diffuser fins and side air curtains streamline airflow, stabilizing the RS 3 at high speeds and reducing lift.
