Compact Supersport

Audi RS 3 unleashed

The Audi RS 3 is the distilled essence of Audi Sport engineering. Its wide stance, quattro all-wheel drive, and charismatic five-cylinder engine combine to deliver a compact performance car with the soul of a rally champion.

Built for drivers who demand precision without sacrificing practicality, the RS 3 delivers daily comfort with a soundtrack that stirs every enthusiast's pulse.

  • 2.5 TFSI five-cylinder turbo with a distinctive warble.
  • Torque splitter for controlled, rear-biased agility.
  • Driver-focused cockpit with Audi Virtual Cockpit plus.

Everyday usability, track-ready response

The RS 3 is engineered to conquer commutes and canyon roads alike. Adaptive dampers absorb rough pavement while RS Drive modes transform the chassis into a razor-sharp companion when you crave exhilaration.

Audi's legendary quattro technology now features a torque splitter that actively distributes power between the rear wheels, creating confident grip on snow and playful rotation on dry tarmac.

Iconic heritage

The odd-fire cadence of the five-cylinder pays homage to Audi's Group B rally dominance while modern enhancements keep the RS 3 at the front of the performance pack.

Technology tuned for drivers

RS-spec displays, telemetry readouts, and customizable drive profiles make the cabin a command center for every journey.

Confidence in all conditions

Whether you're carving snowy switchbacks or setting lap times, the RS 3's all-wheel drive traction delivers confidence when the weather turns challenging.
