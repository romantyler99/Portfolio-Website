Performance engineering

Audi Sport's engineers shaped the RS 3 to be the most charismatic compact performance car on the road. Its award-winning inline five delivers relentless acceleration while the quattro drive system sculpts every corner with confidence.

Beyond pure speed, the RS 3 is defined by responsiveness. Lightweight components, rapid-fire shifts from the seven-speed S tronic gearbox, and RS ceramic brakes create a machine that reacts instantly to driver inputs.

Powertrain & chassis statistics
Specification RS 3 figure
Engine 2.5-liter TFSI five-cylinder turbo
Output 401 hp @ 6,500 rpm / 369 lb-ft from 2,250-5,600 rpm
0-60 mph 3.6 seconds (with launch control)
Driveline Quattro all-wheel drive with torque splitter
Top speed 180 mph with RS Dynamic package
Braking Optional 14.8 in. carbon-ceramic front discs

The RS torque splitter is the heart of the latest RS 3. By sending up to 100 percent of rear axle torque to either wheel, it sharpens turn-in, enhances stability, and unlocks RS Torque Rear mode for controlled drift angles on closed courses.

Launch control mastery

Dual-clutch launches deliver consistent 0-60 sprints thanks to optimized shift programs that keep the turbo within its sweet spot.

Adaptive suspension

RS sport suspension with adaptive dampers monitors each wheel individually, balancing ride comfort with maximum cornering grip.

Exhaust acoustics

Selectable exhaust flaps tune the RS soundtrack from stealthy to spine-tingling, celebrating the five-cylinder rhythm.
